## Short description (under 300 characters)

Cut real speech from your videos into syllables, stitch them into your own lyrics, and make them sing by dragging note blocks. Load a backing track and it even follows the original melody. That unmistakable “stitched” sound — in a few clicks.

## Long description

- 🎬 **Drop in a video** — speech recognition sorts talk into words and syllables (Korean-first; Japanese and English recognition)
- ✍️ **Stitch to your lyrics** — missing words are built from syllables; missing syllables from similar sounds. Clips are re-checked by ear and the clearest piece is chosen
- 🎹 **Move do-re-mi blocks** — stay in key even if you don’t read music. Consonants stay original; only vowels stretch to the beat
- 🎵 **Follow the original** — a backing track supplies tempo, key, and melody for your lyrics
- 🎞️ **Video too** — each syllable’s original shot stretches with the song
- 💾 **Export** — MP4 (720p), WAV, MIDI (with lyrics)
- 🔒 **All on your computer** — no internet, no upload
- 🌐 **UI languages** — Korean, English, Japanese (follows the OS at first; switch from the top right)

**System requirements:** macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon recommended) / Windows 10 or 11 64-bit, 8 GB RAM or more, about 2 GB disk. Time depends on the machine (about 40 minutes to analyze a 1-hour video on M3).

**Notice:** The program includes no video or audio. You are responsible for rights in imported material and in your results. The sound is intentionally “stitched”; this is not a natural singing synthesizer.

## Steam AI content disclosure (Content Survey → AI Generated Content) draft

**Live-Generated Content:**

> DCinVox uses AI models that run locally on the user's computer: speech recognition (OpenAI Whisper, MIT license) to transcribe the speech in videos the user imports, and a phoneme recognition model (wav2vec2, Apache-2.0) to locate syllables. Pitch changes use a conventional signal-processing vocoder (WORLD). The app does not generate new voices or images with generative AI; output is assembled from the user's own imported media. No content is included with the app, and no user data is uploaded. Users are responsible for the rights to the media they import; the app shows a notice about this before every generation and export.

**Guardrails:** copyright notice before every generate and export; no media ships with the app; EULA forbids impersonation and disinformation.
